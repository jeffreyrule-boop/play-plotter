How To Use Falcons Play Plotter

1. 🏈 Select Players on the Field

Tap a player's box to select them for the active play. Tapped boxes light up blue with a blue dot.

2. ⏱️ Quarters & Lineup Status

  • Select the current quarter (Q1–Q4) at the bottom.
  • The banner tells you how many players are selected. Target is 11 players.
  • Once 11 players are highlighted, tap "Lock In Play" to credit everyone on field.

3. ↩️ Undo Last Play

Made a mistake? Tap "Undo" at the bottom to remove the previous play and return the players on field to their exact previous state.

4. 🔄 Syncing to Google Sheets

Tap Menu ▾ and select 🔄 Sync to push game data to the master spreadsheet. The Syncing... spinner appears in the top header while updating.

5. ✏️ Manual Adjustments & Absences

  • Tap Menu ▾ and select ✏️ Edit (or tap the yellow Done Editing button when finished).
  • While editing, tap any player to change their total play count manually or mark them as Absent / Injured / OUT.

6. 👤 Plotter Name & Game Title

Tap your name or game title in the top-left corner anytime to update or rename your tracking session.
