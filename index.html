How To Use Falcons Play Plotter

1. 🏈 Select Players on the Field

Tap a player's box to select them for the active play. Selected boxes glow blue.

2. ⏱️ Quarters & Lineup Status

  • Select current quarter (Q1–Q4).
  • Target is 11 players on field.
  • Tap "Lock In Play" to credit all selected players.

3. ↩️ Undo Last Play

Tap "Undo" to revert the last play recorded.

4. 🔄 Syncing Data

Tap Menu ▾ and select 🔄 Sync to update Google Sheets.

5. ✏️ Manual Adjustments

Use Menu ▾ -> ✏️ Edit to adjust play totals or mark players absent/injured.
